A Proclamation on Payment

HOW WE ACCEPT COIN & CARD
❦

We gladly accept cash, as well as Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and contactless (tap-to-pay) cards.

CashVisaMastercardAmexDiscoverApple PayTap to Pay

As required by Maryland law, a 6% sales tax is due. For those paying in cash, sales tax is included in thy menu price. For those paying by card, sales tax shall be added to thy total.

Our lords and ladies are pleased to serve thee. Tips are not expected, but are certainly appreciated. Dishes are prepared in a shared kitchen and grill area, so trace contact with wheat, milk, soy, egg, peanut, and tree nut is possible even where not named above.
